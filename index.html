02 / THE SOUND

Music that
means something.

These three tracks mark the beginning of a bigger story — each one carrying a different emotion, a different truth, and a different piece of the journey.

01 / TRACK ONE

Allanie’s Anthem

“I Believe”

This song is about youth, resilience and the belief that greatness is already inside us. It speaks to a generation that is often told to stay quiet, stay small and stay within the limits placed on them. But the song says something different: there is power in our dreams, strength in our voice, and potential in every young person willing to rise above fear and doubt.

It is a declaration that every person has what it takes to become who they were meant to be — not by copying the world, but by trusting their own path and refusing to lose themselves.

02 / TRACK TWO

Another One

“Sungankonde Wina”

This is a heartbreak song about deep emotional pain and the confusion of loving someone who can never fully give themselves back. It tells the story of a connection that felt real, a bond that seemed to hold promise, and then the painful truth that the other person has already chosen someone else.

There is heartbreak in the realization that love was not returned in the way it was hoped for. It is a song of disappointment, rejection, and the ache of being left with unanswered feelings and wounded pride.

03 / TRACK THREE

My Melody

A romantic expression of devotion

This track is a warm, heartfelt love song that speaks to the beauty of affection, admiration and genuine connection. It is about a man expressing his love to a woman in a way that feels honest, gentle, and sincere — celebrating her presence, her beauty, and the comfort she brings into his life.

It carries the softness of romance and the sweetness of being completely captivated by someone special. It is a song for the kind of love that feels peaceful, intentional and deeply meaningful.

The journey is only beginning, and each song adds a different chapter to the story.
